What you can expect from me

No pressure, no stiff poses, just a lot of feeling for the moment.

Hi, I'm Emina

I have been passionate about photography and many other creative fields for as long as I can remember. At some point, it became clear to me that I wanted to follow this path professionally too and let everything I have learned and experienced over the past years flow into my work.

Today, my focus is on heartfelt maternity, newborn and family photography. I want to create images that do not feel staged, but show you exactly as you are.

From my own experience, I know how quickly special stages of life change and how valuable it is to hold on to them. That is exactly why it matters so much to me to accompany you in a calm and relaxed atmosphere.

In every session I take my time, respond to you with sensitivity and create space for you to feel comfortable. That is how honest images are created, images that still feel right years later.

Frequently asked questions about me and my work

Here are the key answers if you would like to get to know me and my way of working a little better before inquiring.

Which sessions do I offer?

My focus is on maternity, newborn and family photography. I also cover civil ceremonies, weddings and celebrations. Property, staff, nursery and kindergarten photography is available on request; I am happy to send you the relevant portfolios personally.

Where do sessions take place?

As a rule, all sessions take place in my studio at Mela-Spira-Str. 32b in Graz-Straßgang, unless we arrange an outdoor session or another location of your choice.

Get to know me and my photography personally

If you are looking for calm, natural and high-quality images, I would love to hear from you. Together we can decide which session fits you best.
