Send Emina a message ↗
Do we have to order a package?

No. Registration permits the agreed photographs and your private selection. You place a paid order later in the portal only if you would like to buy images.

How many images will be available?

I plan up to three different individual portraits per child, plus the agreed group photographs featuring your child. The number of successful images also depends on what your child feels comfortable with.

Can I mix portraits and group photographs?

Yes. All images approved for your family count equally: one image costs €10 and each set of three costs €25. For example, two portraits and one group image cost €25 in total, four images €35, and six images €50.

What if my child does not want to take part?

We respect that. A child can watch first, take a break or choose not to be photographed. Smiles are never demanded. There is no obligation to purchase if no suitable images result.

What happens if a child is ill or the weather is bad?

Please let the nursery, kindergarten or me know in good time. We discuss a suitable indoor space or a possible change of date beforehand. An extra visit for individual absent children is not automatically included and needs a separate agreement.

Are sibling or group photographs possible?

Group photographs are planned with the institution. Only children with the appropriate permission may take part. Sibling photographs can be arranged in advance if registration, consent and the schedule allow. Other families do not see your child’s individual portraits.

What should my child wear?

Choose clothes your child feels comfortable in and can move around in. Calm colours often work beautifully. Large logos and busy patterns can distract from their face. A familiar cuddly toy is welcome by arrangement.

Who sees our images? Will they be published?

Individual portraits are kept in a personal family gallery. Agreed group photographs are shown only to authorised families of the children pictured. I publish children’s photographs on my website or Instagram only with separate, voluntary consent for the selected images and channels.

When will we receive the finished files?

The timeframe for the selection gallery and editing is agreed before the photo day and stated in your parent information. Once payment is confirmed and every ordered image is ready, you receive the download email.

Can we print the photographs ourselves?

Yes. Purchased photographs are delivered as fully edited, high-resolution files without watermarks. You may use them for private prints and photo books. Group photographs also involve the rights of the other children pictured: please do not share them publicly or on social media.

How long will the gallery remain available?

The exact closing date is stated in your parent information or gallery. Please download purchased files in good time and keep your own backup. The gallery is not a permanent photo archive.
