3. Privacy choice and browser storage

The website itself sets no HTTP cookies for advertising or visitor recognition. Your optional statistics choice is stored as lizaPrivacyConsent in your browser’s LocalStorage. It contains only the version, statistics on/off and expiry date. The choice expires after 180 days. This storage is necessary to respect your decision and is not used as a visitor identifier. We do not use SessionStorage.

You can change or withdraw your choice through . Without a choice, statistics remain off. If storage is blocked, the choice applies only to the current page. The former lizaLanguage entry is no longer used; you can remove old entries by clearing website data in your browser. The language follows the DE, EN or BS page address.

4. Optional statistics: Cloudflare Web Analytics

After your consent, the homepages may load Cloudflare Web Analytics, provided by Cloudflare, Inc., to measure visits, loading speed and stability. Its script reads technical browser performance data and sends measurements, page information and, where applicable, referring-page information to Cloudflare. Our website does not send form contents to this analytics service.

According to Cloudflare, Web Analytics is cookie-free, uses no LocalStorage or SessionStorage identifiers and creates no persistent visitor identifier. The IP address inherently received with a network connection is discarded at the nearest data centre and is not stored in core RUM databases or logs, according to the provider. Reports are available for up to six months; this does not describe every other retention period of the company.

We load this optional measurement on the basis of consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; section 165(3) Austrian TKG 2021). Without consent, neither the script nor analytics transmissions are initiated. On withdrawal, analytics are stopped and any page with an active beacon reloads. This does not undo earlier lawful transmissions.
