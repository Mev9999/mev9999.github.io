Frequently asked questions about portrait sessions

The key answers to help you feel prepared and comfortable from the start.

Do I need experience in front of the camera?

No. During the session, I show you step by step which posture and expression work well. You do not need to prepare any poses beforehand.

What should I bring to my portrait session?

Bring one or two outfits in which you feel comfortable. Calm colors and clothing without large logos look especially harmonious. Together we choose what best suits the desired image style.
