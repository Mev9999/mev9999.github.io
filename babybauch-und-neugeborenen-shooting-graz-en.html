Frequently asked questions about the maternity and newborn bundle

Everything you need for two relaxed, connected sessions that tell your story from pregnancy to the first days with your baby.

It is best to contact me during pregnancy. This gives us time to plan the maternity session calmly and keep enough flexibility around your due date for the newborn session.

We can schedule the maternity session in advance. For the newborn session, we initially use your estimated due date as a guide and arrange the exact date once your baby has arrived.

The maternity session is often especially lovely between weeks 28 and 34. Newborn images are ideally created during the first 5 to 14 days, always with careful attention to how you and your baby are feeling.

That is completely normal and already considered in the planning. Contact me after the birth and we will flexibly find a suitable date for the second session.

Every package includes two sessions. Depending on the package, you receive 5, 9 or 15 edited images per session and one, up to two or up to three image sets, so the scope is clear from the beginning.

Your partner and siblings are included in the Gold bundle. If shared images are especially important to you, please mention this in your inquiry so we can plan both sessions accordingly.

Both important stages are planned together and photographed in a coherent visual style. The bundle also costs less than two corresponding individual sessions; the current saving is shown transparently on the pricing page.

Absolutely. We can coordinate colours, personal keepsakes and your image wishes so the two series belong together while each stage keeps its own special character.
