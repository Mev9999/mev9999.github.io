Gift vouchers

Good to know

Can I give an entire session?

Yes. You can use my current package prices as a guide. The voucher states your chosen monetary value. The recipient chooses their session later; no particular package or date is booked by buying a voucher.

Which sessions can I use it for?

Personally arranged sessions with me, such as maternity, newborn, family or portrait photography. Organised nursery, kindergarten and school photo days are excluded.

How will I receive my voucher?

Place your order and pay by bank transfer. Once I confirm receipt, I email the voucher PDF to you. There is no postal delivery. You can print it or give it digitally.

How is the voucher redeemed?

The recipient can enter the code in the gift voucher field when enquiring about a session, email it to me, or bring it along. A non-binding enquiry does not use any credit.

What happens to unused credit?

Remaining credit stays on the same voucher. If a session costs more than the available credit, the recipient pays the difference.

How long can it be used?

There is no short expiry period. The statutory limitation period applies. Full terms are provided before and with your order.

Is this the same as a partner code?

No. A partner code provides a discount under its own terms. A gift voucher pays a monetary amount. When you buy a voucher, the value equals the amount you pay.

Can I cancel an online purchase?

Yes. You can withdraw at any time before the activated voucher is first successfully emailed, and for at least 14 days after that. A later statutory start of the period remains unaffected. Sending the PDF alone does not end your withdrawal right. Use your protected order link or email me; see the voucher terms for details.
